Data Protection & Security

TipsyCart Privacy Policy

Your privacy and trust are our top priorities. Here is how we safeguard your personal information.

🔒 1. Information We Collect

When you use TipsyCart to shop, we collect the necessary information required to fulfill your orders safely:

  • Contact Information: Your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address.
  • Order History: Products purchased, order amounts, and delivery timestamps.
  • Authentication: Secure 6-digit one-time password (OTP) verification records. We never store passwords in plaintext.

🛡️ 2. How We Use Your Information

  • To dispatch and deliver your everyday essentials quickly in Kanpur.
  • To send order status updates and OTP verification codes via official email.
  • To improve our hyperlocal catalog and provide customer support.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers.

💳 3. Payment Security

All online payment transactions are processed securely through certified, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways (Razorpay/UPI). TipsyCart never stores your credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank account PINs.

📞 4. Contact Us Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions about our privacy practices or wish to review your data, please contact our support team at support@tipsycart.in.
