📋 Import Guidelines & Data Safety Rules

  • Supported Formats: Microsoft Excel (.xlsx).
  • Column Headers: Product Name *, SKU / Product Code, Brand, Category *, Subcategory, Selling Price (₹) *, MRP (₹), Stock Quantity *, Unit, Unit Value, Status, Description.
  • Automatic Detection: If a row's SKU or Product Name matches an existing store product, it will be marked for Safe Update (updating stock, price, etc.). Otherwise, it will be created as a New Product.
  • Safety Guarantee: Existing products and past sales/orders are never deleted or modified. Cloudinary product images remain intact.
  • Two-Step Verification: Uploading presents a complete validation preview first. Nothing is committed to the database until you click "Confirm Import".
