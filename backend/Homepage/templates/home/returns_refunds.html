Returns & Refunds Policy

Simple, honest doorstep return and replacement terms for Kanpur shoppers.

📦 Doorstep Returns & Replacements

At TipsyCart, we strive to deliver 100% genuine, fresh, and pristine essentials right to your doorstep in Kanpur. If you receive an item that is damaged, defective, or incorrect, you are eligible for an immediate replacement or full refund.

  • Damaged or Expired Products: Inform us within 24 hours of delivery with a quick photo. Our delivery partner will pick up the item and hand you a replacement.
  • Incorrect Item Delivered: Immediate replacement dispatched at zero extra delivery cost.
  • Missing Items from Pack: Instant refund processed for missing quantities or redelivery.

💡 Tip: For rapid resolution, you can WhatsApp our Kanpur dispatch desk or email support@tipsycart.in with your Order ID.

🌸 Personal Care & Hygiene Products

Due to health, sanitation, and safety regulations, intimate hygiene items (such as sanitary pads, tampons, menstrual cups) and opened personal care cosmetics cannot be returned once the protective seal has been broken or tampered with, unless the product arrived damaged or defective.

💳 Refund Timelines & Methods

Once your return is verified, your refund will be processed promptly:

  • UPI / Online Payments: Credited back to the original bank account or UPI ID within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD): Instant UPI transfer or store credit upon pickup verification.

❓ Need Help With a Return?

Visit our Contact Us page or email us directly at support@tipsycart.in with your Order ID.
